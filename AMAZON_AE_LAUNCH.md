# DealLens AI — Amazon.ae First 3 Sales Launch

## What this version does
- Adds 10 original Amazon.ae shopping guides.
- Uses Amazon.ae tagged Special Links with the configured Associate tag.
- Adds required Associate disclosure on the main shopping page and near affiliate links.
- Uses `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` on outbound affiliate links.
- Sends shoppers to Amazon.ae search results rather than inventing product prices or ASINs.
- Keeps live price/product data out of the static site until an approved Amazon product-data integration is available.
- Records outbound-click counts locally in the visitor's browser for basic UX diagnostics; this is not Amazon conversion reporting.

## Important
Amazon determines qualifying purchases and commissions. The site cannot guarantee sales or earnings.

Amazon currently requires at least 3 qualified sales within the first 180 days for application review. Personal orders do not qualify. Amazon also expects robust original content and says a good rule of thumb is at least 10 posts.

## Publishing
Upload the files to the root of the `main` branch of the DealLens AI GitHub Pages repository. Keep the existing Terms and Privacy pages.

## Security
Do not put passwords, bank details, tax identifiers, API keys or other secrets in this repository.
