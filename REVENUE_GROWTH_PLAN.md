# DealLens AI — Revenue growth operating plan (October 2026)

## Objective
Build a sustainable, scalable US-first buying-research business with globally useful content. High revenue is a target, **not a forecast**. Traffic, advertiser approval, affiliate approval, clicks, conversion and earnings have not been verified.

## Revenue model and constraints
1. **Affiliate:** help visitors make informed purchases; use only verified, eligible affiliate programs and accurately disclosed tracking links. A tracking ID appearing in code does not establish enrollment, approval or payment eligibility. Do not add unverified tracking to new markets.
2. **Advertising:** monetize qualifying content if and when AdSense or another ad network approves the site and actual ad serving is confirmed. The existing publisher script does not prove approval.
3. **Future direct partnerships:** only after traffic, audience and merchant relationships justify them; disclose sponsored placements clearly and preserve editorial independence.

## Prioritized execution
- **P0: Confirm monetization status.** Check approval and policy standing for each affiliate market (US, UAE, UK, Canada) and AdSense. Record evidence, date and allowed site domains; never assume approval.
- **P0: Confirm the actual canonical site.** Root `agel-uk.github.io` and project path `/deallens-ai/` are distinct. Verify the domain/site being reviewed in AdSense, index coverage and page analytics.
- **P1: Improve conversion without deceptive claims.** Build use-case pathways (home office, travel, home essentials, fitness) to useful original guides, then comparison worksheets, then retailer searches. Do not claim independent product testing or show invented live prices.
- **P1: Measure the funnel with consent-aware analytics once configured.** Track aggregate page entrances, guide-to-tool clicks, outbound retailer clicks and search impressions; avoid storing raw search queries or personal data without a reviewed privacy policy.
- **P1: Publish original, high-intent content.** For each new page, answer a distinct buyer question with compatibility checks, realistic ownership-cost trade-offs, safety considerations and current references where necessary. Avoid mass-generated thin pages or fake “best” rankings.
- **P2: Test improvements.** Compare guide engagement and qualified outbound click-through before scaling a design. Investigate pages with impressions but low click-through, and fix content gaps.
- **P2: Expand markets only after the first market's editorial and commercial workflows are validated.** Localize meaningfully, not by superficial keyword swaps.

## Financial model
Illustrative monthly affiliate revenue = eligible outbound clicks × merchant-reported purchase conversion rate × average qualifying order value × applicable earned commission rate. Actual commissions depend on each program's rules, returns, attribution and exclusions. Advertising revenue is separate and requires approved ad serving and measured traffic. **Do not present hypothetical calculations as earned income.**

## Release checklist
- One useful search intent and original helpful content per landing page.
- Accurate canonical, sitemap, internal links and responsive navigation.
- Clear disclosure and no unsupported “live,” “tested,” “best” or approval claims.
- Relevant internal route: guide → evidence-based comparison → retailer.
- Static checks pass, then browser/manual mobile tests before claiming a fully verified experience.
