## Post 1 — work-from-home headphone decision

**Suggested title:** Choosing headphones for work calls? Check the microphone before ANC specs.

**Useful standalone text:** A headphone that sounds great for music can still perform poorly on a noisy Zoom or Teams call. Before buying, look for mic samples recorded with background noise, check multipoint switching between laptop and phone, and compare long-session comfort. ANC reduces noise for the listener; microphone noise suppression affects the people on the call. For travel, also check wired fallback and battery life with ANC enabled.

**Optional relevant reference, only if allowed:** https://agel-uk.github.io/deallens-ai/guide-wireless-headphones.html

## Post 2 — robot vacuums for pet owners

**Suggested title:** Pet hair and carpet? Four robot-vacuum checks before buying.

**Useful standalone text:** For shedding pets, check how easily the main brush can be cleaned, whether replacement parts are available, and whether the robot avoids pet toys or cables. For mixed carpet and hard floors, confirm carpet detection and mop-lift or no-mop zones. Self-emptying docks save handling but introduce consumable costs. Check the exact model and return policy.

**Optional relevant reference, only if allowed:** https://agel-uk.github.io/deallens-ai/guide-robot-vacuums.html

## Post 3 — mesh Wi-Fi in larger homes

**Suggested title:** Before buying mesh Wi-Fi for a two-story home, map the dead zones.

**Useful standalone text:** Node count is not a guarantee of coverage. Start with the modem location, construction materials and where signal drops. Wired Ethernet backhaul can be valuable when available. Compare subscription requirements, device compatibility and realistic node placement before paying extra for Wi-Fi 7.

**Optional relevant reference, only if allowed:** https://agel-uk.github.io/deallens-ai/guide-mesh-wifi.html
