EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY

Decision criteria before product hype.

DealLens is an independent buying-guidance project designed to help shoppers understand trade-offs before they open retailer results.

Our starting point: the use case

Each guide begins with the job a buyer needs the product to perform. We then identify constraints such as compatibility, dimensions, comfort, maintenance, recurring cost, warranty and return terms.

What counts as useful information

We prioritize criteria that can change the purchase decision. A specification is useful when it affects real use; a long feature list is not useful by itself. We explain trade-offs instead of declaring a universal winner.

What we do not claim

The current static site does not present retailer prices as live data, does not claim to have tested every product, and does not manufacture ratings, commissions or earnings. Current price, seller, stock, delivery and product-specific claims must be verified on the retailer page.

Affiliate independence

Some outbound retailer links contain approved affiliate tracking. DealLens may receive a commission from qualifying purchases without increasing the shopper's price. Affiliate availability does not determine the core checklist or selection criteria in a guide.

Market routing

The market selector routes shoppers to supported Amazon marketplaces. An affiliate tag is appended only when an approved tracking ID has been configured for that market.

Updates and corrections

Buying criteria can change as standards, devices and retailer policies evolve. We revise guidance when a category changes materially or when an error is identified.

Reader responsibility

DealLens provides general educational buying guidance. Readers should verify compatibility, safety instructions, warranty, seller identity, return terms and current commercial information before purchasing.

How to evaluate the evidence behind a buying decision

DealLens is a research and decision-support site, not a hands-on testing laboratory. Unless a page explicitly documents a test method and results, its comparisons are editorial frameworks rather than measured product rankings. We do not claim to have purchased or independently tested every product discussed.

Three levels of information

Evidence typeHow readers should use it
Manufacturer specificationsCheck the exact model number, region and revision; published specifications may not capture real-world behavior.
Independent measurementsPrefer sources that disclose equipment, methods and limitations; compare tests only when their conditions are similar.
Illustrative scenarios and calculationsTreat examples as decision aids, not verified performance or price claims for a particular product.

Before clicking a retailer link

Write down your intended use, non-negotiable compatibility requirements, total budget including tax and accessories, and the retailer's return terms. Use our comparison worksheet to record your own evidence and our ownership-cost calculator to estimate longer-term expenses. Neither tool accesses live retailer prices.

What we do not guarantee

We do not guarantee a product's current availability, pricing, suitability for a specific person or continued software support. Readers should verify consequential safety, electrical and travel restrictions with the relevant manufacturer, carrier or authority.
