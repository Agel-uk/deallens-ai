EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY

Useful criteria before product hype.

DealLens is designed to help shoppers identify the factors that matter before opening retailer results.

How our guides are built

Each guide starts with a use case rather than a brand ranking. We focus on compatibility, fit, ownership cost, maintenance, return terms, warranty, and the specifications that materially affect everyday use.

What DealLens does not claim

The current static version does not present retailer prices as live data, does not claim to have tested every product, and does not manufacture commission or earnings figures. Current price, seller, stock, delivery and product-specific claims must be verified on the retailer page.

Affiliate independence

Some outbound retailer links can contain approved affiliate tracking. DealLens may receive a commission from qualifying purchases without increasing the shopper's price. Affiliate availability does not change the core checklist in a buying guide.

Global markets

The market selector routes shoppers to the appropriate Amazon marketplace supported by DealLens. An affiliate tag is appended only when an approved tracking ID has been configured for that market.

Corrections and updates

Buying criteria can change as standards, devices and retailer policies evolve. Guides should be reviewed when major compatibility standards or product categories change.
