Fit, repeatability and care matter more than headline claims.

For shoes and wearables, start with the specific activity and conditions. Check fit or sizing guidance, materials, water resistance definitions, charging or care needs, and whether the item can be returned after trying it on.

Separate manufacturer specifications from personal-fit questions: a specification cannot tell you whether a shoe feels comfortable on your foot.
