Choose by daily workload, not by the longest feature list.

For appliances, estimate how often the product will run, where it will be stored, and how much cleaning or upkeep is acceptable. For robot vacuums, map thresholds and floor types. For air fryers, measure the usable basket and counter clearance. For coffee equipment, compare ongoing filter, capsule or bean costs.

Compare total ownership cost, available service and replacement parts, the seller’s return terms, and dimensions with doors or lids fully open.
