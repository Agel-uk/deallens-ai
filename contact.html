Corrections and editorial feedback.

DealLens welcomes reports about broken links, outdated buying criteria or unclear guidance.

What to include

When reporting an issue, identify the guide and the specific statement or link that needs review. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive account data.

Response scope

DealLens provides general buying guidance and cannot handle retailer orders, refunds, delivery disputes or warranty claims. Contact the retailer or manufacturer for transaction-specific support.

Repository

Technical site issues can also be reported through the public DealLens AI GitHub repository.
