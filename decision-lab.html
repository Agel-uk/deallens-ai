GLOBAL 2.0 • PRIVATE DECISION WORKSHEET

Choose by evidence, not hype.

Compare three products using your own priorities. All scoring happens in your browser. No live price feeds, fabricated ratings, or paid AI services.

Preview feature: enter your own verified specifications and prices. Scores are subjective decision aids, not independent product tests.

STEP 1

Set your priorities

Move each priority slider from 0 (not important) to 5 (essential).

STEP 2

Enter products and evidence

Rate each product from 1–5 only after reviewing trusted specifications, hands-on reviews, warranty terms and return policies. Unknown values should stay blank.

STEP 3

Your comparison

Add at least two named products and score their criteria to see a comparison. Products with missing evidence are marked as incomplete.

How the decision score works

Each criterion contributes its priority weight multiplied by your product rating. The final score is the weighted average, scaled to 100. Missing ratings are excluded and clearly reduce evidence coverage. Prices are entered by you; total cost includes optional shipping and estimated fees, not automatically calculated tax.

Products above your budget are flagged, not silently removed. Higher scores are not guarantees of quality or suitability.
