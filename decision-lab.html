PRIVATE DECISION WORKSHEET

Compare products using your own priorities

Enter up to three products, score their verified features and compare weighted results. Calculations run in your browser; this worksheet does not fetch live prices or claim to test products.

Preview tool: your ratings are subjective decision aids, not independent product tests. Leave unknown ratings blank.

STEP 1

Set your priorities

Move each priority slider from 0 (not important) to 5 (essential).

STEP 2

Enter products and evidence

Use verified specifications, warranty information and reliable reviews. Unknown criteria should remain unassessed.

Your entries stay in this browser unless you choose to save them here.

STEP 3

Your comparison

Enter at least two named products and their evidence-based ratings, then compare.

How the score works

Each rating (1–5) is multiplied by its priority weight (0–5). The weighted average is scaled to 100. Missing ratings are excluded from the score and reduce evidence coverage. A high score with low coverage is less informative than one backed by complete evidence. The tool does not declare a winner when evidence is too incomplete.

Estimated cost is entered price plus optional shipping and fees; tax is not automatically calculated. Products above your budget are flagged, not removed. Results are based solely on your entries and are not endorsements.

Example

If comfort matters more than battery life, set a higher weight for comfort. A product rated 5/5 for comfort and 3/5 for battery life can then outrank a product with the opposite scores. Verify each rating before relying on the result.
