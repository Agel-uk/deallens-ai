EDITORIAL POLICY

Useful guidance before monetization.

Our editorial rules are designed to keep buying criteria independent from affiliate availability.

How topics are selected

We focus on categories where buyers face meaningful trade-offs in compatibility, fit, ownership cost, maintenance or everyday usability.

How we write

Guides explain decision criteria and common mistakes. We avoid presenting retailer prices as live unless a system actually retrieves current data, and we do not claim hands-on testing unless it occurred.

Affiliate relationships

Some retailer links contain approved affiliate tracking. DealLens may receive a commission from qualifying purchases without increasing the shopper's price. A market without an approved tracking ID remains a standard retailer route.

Evidence, examples and update notes

Worked examples are labeled as hypothetical whenever they use illustrative prices, ratings or specifications. We link to relevant primary references when discussing safety rules or technical standards. We do not present invented rankings, unverified product tests or assumed stock levels as facts.

Material editorial changes may carry an addition or review date. A date describes an actual edit, not a guarantee that every product specification has been independently rechecked. Readers can report factual corrections through our contact page.

Corrections

Standards and product categories change. When a factual or structural issue is identified, we aim to correct the relevant guide rather than preserve outdated guidance.

Commercial information

Retailers remain the source for current price, seller, stock, shipping, warranty and return terms. Readers should verify those details before purchase.
