FAQ

How DealLens works.

Does DealLens sell products?

No. DealLens publishes buying guidance and routes shoppers to third-party marketplaces. Purchases, payments, delivery and returns are handled by the retailer.

Are prices shown live?

No. We deliberately avoid inventing or caching prices as if they were current. The retailer page is the source of current price and availability.

Do you earn commissions?

Some links contain approved affiliate tracking. DealLens may earn from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to the shopper.

Does an affiliate relationship change a guide?

It should not. Guides are structured around use cases, constraints, ownership factors and common mistakes rather than commission rates.

Do you test every product?

No. We do not claim hands-on testing unless it actually occurred. The current guides are category-level decision frameworks, not laboratory reviews.

Why are there multiple markets?

Availability, currency, sellers and shipping differ by country. Market routing helps readers open the relevant retailer, while affiliate tracking is added only where an approved public ID is configured.
