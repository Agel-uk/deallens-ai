Air Fryer Buying Guide

Air fryers are compact convection ovens. The best size and design depend on how many people you cook for, what foods you prepare and how much counter space you can dedicate to the appliance.

Choose usable capacity

Basket litres are helpful but do not tell the whole story. A wide basket can fit food in a single layer better than a deep narrow basket. For larger households, dual-basket or oven-style designs may reduce batch cooking.

Counter space and ventilation

Measure the appliance and the space needed around it for ventilation. Also consider where the hot basket will be placed when removed. A model that is too large to leave out may be used less often.

Controls and temperature range

Simple controls can be more useful than many presets. Check the temperature range, timer behavior and whether the machine remembers settings. If you cook different foods together, independent zones can be useful.

Cleaning

Removable dishwasher-safe parts can save time, but check the manufacturer's instructions. Non-stick coatings need appropriate utensils and cleaning. Complex oven-style models may have more surfaces to clean.

Common mistakes

Do not choose capacity only from marketing photos. Compare internal dimensions, basket shape, power requirements, warranty and availability of replacement trays or baskets.
