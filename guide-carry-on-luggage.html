Airline rules come before luggage labels

There is no universal carry-on size. Airlines can set different cabin dimensions, weight limits and fare allowances. Measure the full case including wheels and handles and check each airline before departure.

Hard shell versus soft side

Hard cases may protect contents and resist some moisture; soft cases may offer exterior pockets and flexible packing. Neither design guarantees durability or acceptance at the gate.

Two wheels versus four

Spinner wheels are convenient on smooth terminals; larger two-wheel designs can behave differently on rough pavement. Consider where you actually roll the bag.

Weight and usable packing space

Empty case weight affects your remaining allowance. Compare interior dimensions, compression systems and whether expansion makes the bag exceed cabin limits.

Handles, zippers and repairability

Inspect telescopic-handle stability, wheel mounting, zipper quality and replacement part availability. Long warranties may exclude airline damage or normal wear.

Security and organization

Consider laptop access, liquid pouches, locks and separate compartments. Verify accepted lock standards and avoid assuming a lock makes valuables secure.

Returns and long-term value

Check warranty exclusions, repair process, shipping costs and seller authenticity. Airline size policies may change, so confirm before each trip.

  • Review warranty exclusions and return shipping.

Frequently asked questions

Is carry-on luggage size standardized worldwide?

No. Limits vary by airline, fare and route; always verify current carrier rules.

Do wheels count toward carry-on measurements?

Often yes. Measure the full exterior including wheels and handles.

Are hard-shell suitcases more durable?

Not necessarily. Materials, construction, wheels and handling conditions all matter.

Are spinner wheels good on cobblestones?

Small spinners may struggle on rough ground. Compare wheel size and design for your routes.

Should I buy expandable carry-on luggage?

Expansion adds flexibility but may exceed cabin dimensions when fully packed.
