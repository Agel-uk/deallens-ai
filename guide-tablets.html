Define the role Decide whether the tablet is mainly for video, reading, note-taking, travel, drawing, work or laptop replacement. That choice determines how much performance, storage and accessory support you need. Screen and comfort Compare display size, aspect ratio, brightness and weight. Larger screens improve multitasking and media, while smaller tablets are easier to hold for long reading sessions and travel. Accessories and ecosystem Keyboard cases, styluses and external displays can change what a tablet can do, but they may add substantial cost. Confirm compatibility with the exact tablet generation before buying accessories. Storage and connectivity Check usable storage, cloud options, Wi-Fi generation and whether cellular connectivity is necessary. Some tablets do not support expandable storage, so the initial capacity choice can matter for long-term ownership. Common mistakes Do not assume a tablet can replace every laptop workflow. Verify the apps, file handling, browser features, peripherals and multitasking functions you depend on before paying for a premium model.
