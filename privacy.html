Privacy

Last updated: September 12, 2026.

Current site operation

DealLens AI currently operates primarily as a static website. The market selector and limited outbound-click diagnostics can be stored in your browser's local storage. The current public frontend does not require a DealLens account.

External retailer and affiliate links

When you follow a retailer link, you leave DealLens AI. The destination retailer may process data under its own privacy and cookie policies. Some links can contain an approved affiliate identifier, which can allow the retailer to attribute a qualifying purchase to DealLens.

Advertising and analytics

DealLens does not state that advertising or analytics services are active unless they have actually been deployed. If Google advertising, analytics, consent management or similar services are enabled, this notice and the site's consent controls must be updated before or at deployment to describe the technologies and choices available to visitors.

Product images

In the current static version, local image preview functionality runs in the browser. Server-side AI image analysis is not enabled.

Your browser choices

You can clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. Doing so can reset saved site preferences.

Changes

This policy will be updated when DealLens adds accounts, payments, advertising cookies, analytics, server-side AI or other data-processing features.
