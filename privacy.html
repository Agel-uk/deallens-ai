Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

DealLens AI is a static buying-guide and marketplace-routing website. This policy explains what information may be stored or processed when you use the site, follow affiliate links, or interact with advertising services if they are enabled.

1. Information DealLens AI stores locally

DealLens AI may use your browser's local storage to remember preferences such as your selected marketplace or region and to keep limited local diagnostic information about outbound-link clicks. This information is stored in your browser and is not represented as a confirmed purchase, commission, or retailer transaction.

2. Product images and local browser features

If you use a product-image preview feature, the current static version of DealLens AI is designed to process that preview locally in your browser. DealLens AI does not currently provide a production server-side image-recognition service or upload those images to a DealLens AI server.

3. Affiliate links

Some links on DealLens AI are affiliate links. If you follow an approved affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, DealLens AI may earn a commission. This does not increase the price you pay. Retailers and affiliate networks may use cookies, tracking parameters, or similar technologies to attribute referrals and purchases under their own privacy policies and terms.

4. External websites

DealLens AI links to third-party websites, including online retailers. Once you leave DealLens AI, the privacy practices, cookies, prices, availability, account systems, and transaction processing of the destination website are controlled by that third party. Review the privacy policy of the destination service before providing personal information or making a purchase.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

DealLens AI may use Google AdSense or other advertising services. When Google advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to DealLens AI or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

If additional third-party advertising vendors or ad networks are enabled, they may also use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies. DealLens AI will update this policy or its consent controls when additional advertising services are introduced.

6. Consent for users in the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

When advertising is served to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, DealLens AI will use an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform where required for Google publisher products. The consent interface may allow users to accept, reject, or manage relevant advertising and privacy choices before or while ads are served.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

DealLens AI itself is currently a static site and does not require an account to read its editorial content. Browser storage may be used for site preferences. Affiliate partners, advertising providers, and other third parties reached through or embedded in the site may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

8. Analytics

DealLens AI does not claim that a production analytics service is active unless one is actually enabled. If analytics services are added later, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated to describe the data processing involved.

9. Accounts, payments, and sensitive information

The current public GitHub Pages version of DealLens AI does not provide production user accounts, merchant billing, or direct payment processing. Do not submit passwords, payment-card data, banking credentials, or other sensitive information through static site fields. If these services are added in the future, they will require secure production infrastructure and updated privacy disclosures.

10. Children's privacy

DealLens AI is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through the current static site. Users should follow the age and account requirements of any third-party retailer or advertising service they visit.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when DealLens AI adds or changes advertising, analytics, accounts, payments, server-side AI, affiliate partners, or other data-processing features. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

12. Privacy resources

Users can review Google's privacy information and advertising controls directly through Google, including Google's Ads Settings and privacy documentation. Questions about DealLens AI can be raised through the project's public GitHub repository.
