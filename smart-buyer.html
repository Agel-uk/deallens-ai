    Why specifications matter more than a headline capacity

    mAh measures battery charge, not guaranteed energy delivered to your device. Check the manufacturer's watt-hour (Wh) rating, USB-C Power Delivery (PD) profiles, single-port output and combined output when multiple devices are plugged in.

    For flights, check your airline's current spare lithium-battery policy. Power banks normally belong in cabin baggage, not checked baggage; larger batteries can require approval or be prohibited.
