WORK FROM HOME

Build a productive desk setup

Prioritize ergonomic fit, screen readability and compatibility before buying.

  • For laptops, check memory, ports, software requirements and repairability.
  • For monitors, match resolution and text clarity to your viewing distance.
  • For chairs, prioritize adjustability and return terms over appearance.

TRAVEL & COMMUTING

Pack reliable everyday tech

Consider weight, charging compatibility and airline restrictions.

  • For headphones, balance comfort, microphone quality and noise control.
  • For power banks, verify USB-C output and carrier battery limits.
  • For carry-ons, check your actual airline's dimensions and weight rules.

HOME ESSENTIALS

Spend less time on household chores

Estimate ongoing effort, maintenance and space before buying.

  • For robot vacuums, map thresholds, floor types and replacement parts.
  • For air fryers, consider actual usable basket area and cleaning.
  • For coffee makers, compare per-cup consumables and daily cleanup.

Match gear to your routine

Compatibility and comfort usually matter more than feature counts.
